My Top 3 Movies of All Time

1. Top Gun

Top Gun is a classic action film that has stood the test of time. With its thrilling aerial sequences and iconic soundtrack, it remains a favorite among movie enthusiasts.

2. Uri: The Surgical Strike

Uri: The Surgical Strike is a gripping war drama that highlights the bravery and sacrifice of Indian soldiers. The film is known for its realistic portrayal of military operations and strong performances.

3. The Meg

The Meg is a science fiction horror film that follows a team of deep-sea explorers who encounter a massive prehistoric shark. The movie is known for its stunning visual effects and intense action sequences.
